Results

fair test at equal quality

Every scheme's strength was tuned per image until the watermarked picture had exactly PSNR 40 dB, then all 11 attacks were run. The held-out images were never used while designing the proposed scheme, so they are the honest test.

009

Why it's better

the theory

Single-transform schemes put each bit in one kind of coefficient, so the attack that destroys that coefficient destroys the watermark. The proposed scheme uses diversity from communication theory: three carriers that fail under different attacks, combined by a blind, reliability-weighted vote.
